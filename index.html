01 / About

The person behind the mic

Hi, I'm Lucy! I'm a junior studying Broadcast and Digital Journalism at Syracuse University, and I work where broadcast meets social.

On campus, I deliver the weather on Juice & Java, run social media and marketing for Orange Television Network, DJ on WJPZ Z-89 and host my own food series, Cooking with Cuse. Off campus, I've interned with the National Inventors Hall of Fame and CNY Women's Network, where I make videos and write blog posts spotlighting women-owned businesses.

I like taking a project from idea to publish: writing the script, shooting in the field, editing, and then working out how to get people to actually watch it. Fast-paced, collaborative teams are where I do my best work.
